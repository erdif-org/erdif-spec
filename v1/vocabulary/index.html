    erdif: vocabulary¶

    This page is the reference of the ERDIF namespace. Terms reused from schema.org are not listed here — see their definitions directly at schema.org.

    Classes¶

    Advert¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Advert

    Root entity of the format. Represents a single real estate offer.

    Price¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Price

    A single price item of the offer. An offer may carry several of different types.

    Contact¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Contact

    Contact entity of the offer. Kept separate from property data so personal data can be removed or anonymised independently.

    Realty¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Realty

    The physical property. Kept separate from the commercial part because its description is independent of how it is currently advertised.

    Building¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Building

    The building the property belongs to. Its attributes are shared by all units in the building, so they are kept apart from the unit itself.

    Location¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Location

    Location of the property combining three independent means of identification.

    InspireIdentifier¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#InspireIdentifier

    Supranational INSPIRE identifier of a spatial object (address, building or cadastral parcel).

    LocalIdentifier¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#LocalIdentifier

    Identifier from a national registry. Passed through unchanged for verification in local registries.

    LocalArea¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#LocalArea

    An amenity in the surroundings of the property. Kept separate from Location, because it is descriptive information, not spatial identification.

    Room¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Room

    A room of the property. Layout and total usable area can be derived from rooms.

    Equipment¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Equipment

    An item of equipment bound to a specific room.

    UtilityNetwork¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#UtilityNetwork

    Connection of the property to a utility network. Network and connection are two independent axes, hence two codelists.

    Parking¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Parking

    A parking option belonging to the property. Not modelled as a room, because parking does not count towards the layout or usable area of the unit.

    Media¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#Media

    A multimedia item of the offer, including display order.

    ExportInformation¶

    https://erdif-org.github.io/erdif-spec/ns/v1#ExportInformation

    Audit trail enabling record matching on repeated import across systems.
